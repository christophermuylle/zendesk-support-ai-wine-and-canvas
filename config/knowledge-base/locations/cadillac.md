# Cadillac, MI

Source: wineandcanvas.com/michigan/cadillac/ plus the team's actual
Zendesk macros (pulled 2026-09-09), which confirm Cadillac is treated as
a normal private-events market (grouped with Tampa/Fort Myers/
Indianapolis/Fort Lauderdale in the pricing tiers) rather than something
special-cased - it just doesn't appear to have its own public event
calendar page.

## Pricing
Private events: standard $39/person (8-29 guests), corporate/business
$44/person (8-29 guests), with volume discounts at 30-49 and 50+ guests -
see shared.md's Private/corporate events section for the full tier
table. Still don't quote an exact number to the customer - point them to
a quote from the team, since group size and standard-vs-corporate change
the price.

## Venue
Mobile studio - they travel to the customer's location. Based in
Cadillac, MI 49601.

## Private events
Group minimum: 8 people. Non-refundable deposit required (transferable up
to 1 year). Standard booking window is 3 hours (2 hours available with
time restrictions). A $50 late fee applies if the event starts more than
an hour behind schedule. Custom painting requests: additional $50 flat
fee. 3 complimentary no-shows allowed above the 8-person minimum.

## What's included
Art supplies, table covers, and aprons. Food/beverages not provided
unless held at a venue partner location.
