# Fort Lauderdale / Miami, FL

Source: wineandcanvas.com/florida/fort-lauderdale/ plus the team's actual
Zendesk macros (pulled 2026-09-09). The macros settle the earlier
open question in config/locations.yaml: Fort Lauderdale shows up
consistently across the team's own private-event pricing tiers,
restaurant partner lists, and promo follow-up emails, right alongside
Tampa/Fort Myers/Indianapolis - so treat it as a normal centrally
supported location, not a special case. (Miami itself is grouped with
Orlando/Naples in pricing rather than with Fort Lauderdale - see below.)

## Pricing
Public events range $34-$70 depending on event/ticket type.

Private events: standard $39/person (8-29 guests), corporate/business
$44/person (8-29 guests), with volume discounts at 30-49 and 50+ guests
- see shared.md's Private/corporate events section. If the customer
specifically says Miami rather than Fort Lauderdale, the private-event
rate is the Orlando/Naples tier instead ($45 standard / $50 corporate) -
don't guess which applies if it's ambiguous, ask.

## Booking
Direct link: https://wineandcanvas.com/florida/fort-lauderdale/
Book through the event listing's "Get Tickets" button - each event has its
own registration page.

## Venues (examples - always confirm the specific one on the event listing)
Pompano Beach Brewing (Pompano Beach), Unseen Creatures (Miami), Tripping
Animals Taproom (Oakland Park), Laudy Bar (Fort Lauderdale). Private
events available "all around town."

## Private events
8-person group minimum for private events (see the flagged note in
shared.md about this figure varying across macros). Don't quote an exact
per-person price - point the customer to a quote from the team.
